      How this map works

      Every dot is one person in the dataset. Pick an attribute in "Group by" — Institution, Program, DBER Field, or PhD Era — and people sharing that value get pulled toward a shared hub and colored together.

      Click a hub (or a legend row) to isolate it — everyone else dims out. While isolated, use "Sub-group isolated set by" to re-cluster just that slice by a second attribute — e.g. isolate Purdue University, then sub-group by DBER Field — a second lens on one slice of the data.

      Before you read too much into the pattern

      • Lines show a shared attribute, not a confirmed relationship — two people linked to the same hub may never have met.
      • Only a small share of DBER Field tags are hand-sourced; most shown are machine-inferred (dashed chips) and unverified.
      • PhD year is known for a small minority of people — most of the graph has no PhD-era group.
      • A large share of people were added via automated co-author mining, not hand-vetted.

      Full detail on sourcing and known gaps: About this project.
